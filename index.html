The Mechanics

How It Works

$FROG is an ERC-20 token wired one-to-one to frog ownership through a Uniswap v4 hook. No off-chain server ever touches your frogs.

01

Buy → Mint

Buy $FROG from the pool and frogs mint straight to your wallet with fresh, randomly rolled traits. No claim step, no allowlist.

02

Hold → Own

Your frogs sit in your wallet as long as you hold the underlying $FROG balance. Full on-chain SVG, viewable with a free eth_call.

03

Sell → Burn

Sell back into the pool and the corresponding frogs burn. Their traits are gone — buy back in later and you reroll from scratch.

04

Transfer → Move

Wallet-to-wallet transfers move the actual frogs, traits intact. No mint, no burn — just the frogs changing hands.

Art and metadata are generated entirely by the contracts — no server, no IPFS images, no metadata API. Every frog you see is rendered live, on-chain, powered by a Uniswap v4 hook on Robinhood Chain.

FAQ

What chain is this on?

Robinhood Chain. That's it — the answer isn't more complicated than that.

How do I see my frogs?

Every frog is rendered fully on-chain as SVG. A free eth_call against the contract returns the live image and traits — no server, no metadata API, no IPFS gateway required.

What happens when I sell?

Selling $FROG back into the pool burns the corresponding frogs. Their traits are gone for good. Buy back in later and you mint fresh frogs with a brand-new random roll.

What are the legendary odds?

Every mint has a 0.5% chance of landing one of 25 one-of-a-kind legendary frogs. Only one copy of each legendary can exist at any given time. Burn a legendary and its design goes back into the pool for someone else to roll.

Is there a team, a roadmap, a presale?

No team section, by design. No presale, no whitelist, no admin key after setup. The contracts are the whole project.

Is the art stored on IPFS or a server?

No. Traits, layering, and rendering all live in the contracts. Nothing is fetched from an external host to display a frog.
